Berita & Regulasi Terbaru

UNDANG-UNDANG NOMOR 22 TAHUN 2009

Pasal 99 Ayat 1 :
"Every planned construction of activity centers, settlements, and infrastructure that will cause disturbances to Security, Safety, Order, and Smooth Traffic and Road Transportation is required to conduct a traffic impact analysis."

Pasal 99 Ayat 3 :
"The results of the traffic impact analysis as referred to in paragraph (1) constitute one of the requirements for developers to obtain permits from the Government and/or Local Government according to laws and regulations."

Traffic impact analysis stated in Law Number 22 of 2009 concerning Traffic and Road Transportation in Article 100 Paragraph 1 also mentions that as referred to in Article 99 paragraph (1) is conducted by a consultant institution that has certified experts.

And Hervian Handika Sugasta, S.T., M.T. as the service provider has completed education and training in Compiling Traffic Impact Analysis for 9 days (70 JP) organized by POLITEKNIK TRANSPORTASI DARAT BALI located in Gianyar and was also declared to have Passed the Competency Test/Certified Traffic Impact Analysis Compiler Expert based on the Decree of the Director General of Land Transportation.
